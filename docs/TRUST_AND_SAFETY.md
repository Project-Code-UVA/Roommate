# Trust & Safety Policy (Internal)

---

# 1. Age Enforcement
18+ required.
Underage accounts must be removed immediately.

---

# 2. Messaging Safety

- No shared-school bypass
- DM bans prevent new conversations
- Blocked users fully hidden

---

# 3. Report Categories

- Harassment
- Sexual content
- Hate speech
- Spam
- Impersonation
- Underage
- Safety threat

---

# 4. Escalation Framework

1. Warning
2. 48h DM ban
3. 7-day suspension
4. Permanent ban

All actions logged.
